# Evidence Research Loop v2.6.0

ERL v2.6.0 adds an empirical Composition Graph for systems whose acceptance depends on verified sub-capabilities and their actual integration.

## Added

- Immutable claims and independent submissions.
- AND premises and alternative OR paths.
- Conditional reductions that do not count as full system acceptance.
- Exact child-evidence and artifact binding before integration.
- Frozen composition, integration, and holdout verifiers.
- Evidence revocation and dependent-path recomputation.
- Audited asset export, import, target-side revalidation, and lexical discovery.
- Read-back and milestone records with explicit identity and independence limitations.
- Eleven MCP tools for composition initialization, submission, status, audit, recovery, revocation, asset handling, and review records.
